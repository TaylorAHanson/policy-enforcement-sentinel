## What was in it

- Scheduled scans of Databricks workspaces, evaluating discovered resources
  against Rego policies with OPA.
- A policy editor with Monaco, syntax validation, and pull requests to GitHub.
- An allowlist for exempting specific resources from specific policies.
- Email notification of scan results.
- A dashboard of scan runs and their violations.

## Known limitations at the time

Policies could request a `KILL` action that terminated or deleted a resource,
gated only by a single configuration flag. That is the thing 1.0.0 replaces.
